Alpha pricing: choose a machine and its rate

Alpha 定价：选择主机与价格

Alpha pricing. Each machine has its own monthly USD rate in the public slot list. Alpha pricing passes through recorded VPS and exit-IP costs, plus a monthly service fee of 10% of resource cost, with a $2 minimum per slot. Your Claude subscription or Anthropic API usage is billed separately.

Recorded costs are normalized to a calendar month. A 30-day cost is multiplied by 365/360; an annual cost is divided by 12. For a customer quote, round the normalized USD resource cost to cents, half up; calculate 10% of that amount, round the service fee half up to cents with a $2 minimum, then add the two displayed amounts. Resource costs of $8.68, $10.83, $22.28 and $27.44 therefore give totals of $10.68, $12.83, $24.51 and $30.18 per month. The fee remains $2 through $20 of resource cost.

Alpha 定价。每台主机的美元月费分别列于公开槽位列表。按已记录的 VPS 与出口 IP 成本计价，另收资源成本 10% 的月度服务费，每槽位最低 $2。你自己的 Claude 订阅或 Anthropic API 用量另行付费。

已记录成本统一折算为日历月：30 天成本乘以 365/360，年付成本除以 12。报价先将折算后的美元资源成本四舍五入到美分，再取其 10% 并将服务费四舍五入到美分，服务费最低 $2，最后将两项显示金额相加。资源成本 $8.68、$10.83、$22.28 和 $27.44，分别对应月费 $10.68、$12.83、$24.51 和 $30.18。资源成本不超过 $20 时，服务费为 $2。

The public breakdown shows VPS cost, separately recorded exit-IP cost, service fee and total. When hosting includes the exit IP, it shows the recorded bundled resource cost without inventing a split. A normalization rounding line explains any cent-level difference between individually rounded components and the rounded combined resource cost. Missing costs or costs not converted to USD make pricing unavailable. The host and IP-provider brands remain visible. ISP and Residential describe network types, with the same pricing formula and no separate residential premium; neither guarantees quality, speed or Claude compatibility.

公开明细展示 VPS 成本、单独记录的出口 IP 成本、服务费与总价。主机包含出口 IP 时，展示已记录的打包资源成本，不虚构拆分金额。若各项单独舍入后与合计资源成本存在美分差异，会以折算舍入行解释。成本缺失或尚未转换为美元时，价格暂不可用。主机与 IP 服务商品牌保持可见。ISP 与住宅只描述网络类型，两者采用同一公式，不另加住宅溢价；这些类型不保证质量、速度或 Claude 兼容性。

Alpha — Early Access. The service is still being tested and has not launched commercially. The operator has personally used one residential slot with encouraging results; reliability across providers, configurations and multiple users is still being evaluated. Alpha pricing is introductory and may change for future billing periods. You will receive an updated quote before renewal if pricing changes; an already agreed billing period retains its agreed price.

Alpha — 早期体验。服务仍在测试，尚未正式商业发布。运营者亲自使用过一个住宅槽位，结果令人鼓舞，但不同服务商、配置及多用户场景的可靠性仍待评估。Alpha 价格为初期价格，未来计费周期可能调整。如有变更，续期前会提供更新报价；已约定计费周期仍执行原约定价格。

Agree the price and paid period with the operator; the website does not charge automatically, renew automatically or rewrite past payments.

价格及服务有效期直接与运营者约定；网站不会自动扣款、自动续费，也不会改写过去的付款记录。

Retired machines are removed from the rental catalogue. A temporarily offline machine that remains in service still appears on Status. Retirement does not release an existing holder or delete their account and payment history.

已退役主机会从出租目录移除。仍在运营、只是暂时离线的主机继续显示在服务状态中。退役不会自动归还已有持有者的槽位，也不会删除其账号或付款历史。

Each customer receives a dedicated VPS and dedicated exit IP. An unallocated slot is labelled Available; this describes availability, not a zero price. From Your slots, open Browse available slots to choose a priced machine and agree the quote with the operator.

每位客户获得专属 VPS 与专属出口 IP。未分配的槽位标为可用，表示可领取，不表示免费。从我的槽位打开浏览可用槽位，选择可用且已定价的主机，并与运营者确认报价。

The user guide explains setup and the displayed quote. Terms describe agreed periods and slot access.

使用指南说明安装和报价明细，服务条款说明约定周期与槽位访问。

Your slots keeps your held slots, controls and usage together. To add a slot, choose Browse available slots. This signed-in page lets you search by host provider, IP provider or public machine label such as Machine 7, and filter by provider and network type (ISP or Residential). Results appear in pages of up to 20 machines. Review a machine’s price and cost breakdown, then claim that machine; you return to Your slots to manage it. Your allowance and the existing eligibility checks still apply, and claiming does not charge you automatically.

我的槽位集中展示你持有的槽位、操作和用量。需要增加槽位时，选择浏览可用槽位。此页面需要登录，可以按主机服务商、IP 服务商或“主机 7”等公开主机标签搜索，并按服务商及网络类型（ISP 或住宅）筛选；每页最多展示 20 台主机。先核对主机价格与成本明细，再领取该主机，随后返回“我的槽位”管理。原有槽位额度及领取条件仍然适用，领取不会自动扣款。
