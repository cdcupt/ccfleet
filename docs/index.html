Two ways to pay for a Fleet slot槽位的两种付费方式

One month with Fleet credit. On Your slots, choose Add credit, select $10, $20, $50 or $100, then Continue to secure payment. Pay on Stripe and check your balance after payment is confirmed. Choose a slot and confirm its separate purchase. This buys one calendar month; renewal needs your confirmation. Adding credit does not buy or renew a slot.

用 Fleet 余额购买一个月。在我的槽位点击充值，选择 10、20、50 或 100 美元，再点击前往安全付款页面。到 Stripe 付款，确认后核对余额。随后另行选择槽位并确认购买，只支付一个日历月，需要时再自行确认续费。充值本身不会购买或续费槽位。

Optional monthly card payment. When a new slot offers Pay monthly, review the USD price, tick the monthly-payment agreement and complete the first payment on Stripe. The next bill follows the paid period. Each slot has an independent plan: two slots mean two monthly payments. This option does not use Fleet credit. Existing slots, balances and prepaid time are not enrolled or converted automatically.

可选的银行卡月付。新槽位提供按月付款时，先核对美元月费，勾选同意每月付款，再到 Stripe 完成首笔付款。下次账单接在已付费服务期之后。每个槽位独立付费，两个槽位就是两份月付计划。此方式不使用 Fleet 余额，也不会自动转换已有槽位、余额或预付费服务期。

For a confirmed monthly plan, open Monthly payments and choose Cancel renewal on that slot. Once confirmed, cancellation stops the next renewal and keeps the paid period. A pending cancellation is not yet confirmed. Payment review pauses new connections; you can still request cancellation. The general Stripe portal is paused while a review or cancellation is pending. Expiry does not automatically wipe files. Cancel billing and wait for paid time to end before explicitly returning the slot. Your own Claude or Devin plan is paid separately. Alipay is not available for Fleet credit. See Terms.

月付计划确认后，可在月付管理为对应槽位选择停止续费。取消确认后不再续下一个月，已付费服务仍保留到期；正在确认取消表示结果尚未确认。付款核查会暂停新连接，但仍可申请取消；核查或取消结果待确认期间，暂停通用 Stripe 管理入口。到期不会自动删除文件；如需归还槽位，请先取消月付，等已付费服务期结束后再明确确认归还。你自己的 Claude 或 Devin 订阅需要另付。Fleet 余额暂不支持支付宝，规则见服务条款。

Only one unfinished monthly checkout can be open at a time; paid plans remain independent. In Monthly payments, choose Continue checkout or Cancel checkout before starting another. Unpaid checkout closes only after Stripe confirms expiry. If payment already completed, you keep that paid month and confirmed cancellation stops future renewal; no automatic cash refund is issued. Keep checking a pending checkout instead of starting a replacement.

一次只能有一笔未完成的月付结账，已付费的计划仍各自独立。在“月付管理”选择继续付款或取消这次付款，再开始另一笔。未付款的页面只有在 Stripe 确认过期后才会关闭；若已经付款，已付费月份仍保留，取消确认后停止今后的续费，不会自动退款。结果待确认时请继续核对同一笔，不要另开一笔。

Alpha pricing: choose a machine and its rate

Alpha 定价：选择主机与价格

The homepage’s Slots & pricing section shows up to three real configuration examples. Open the full public slot catalogue for machine providers, network types, current availability and Alpha price breakdowns. Search host/IP-provider brands or a public label such as Machine 7, filter by provider and IP type, and browse pages of up to 20 machines. The purple Residential, blue ISP and gray Datacenter badges name network types; their text and color do not rank quality or guarantee Claude compatibility. Browsing does not reserve a slot. Sign in and use Browse available slots to choose a machine, review its monthly quote and confirm the purchase shown for your account.

首页“槽位与价格”展示最多三个实际配置示例。打开完整公开槽位目录可查看主机服务商、网络类型、当前可用状态与 Alpha 报价明细。可按主机／IP 服务商或“主机 7”等公开标签搜索，按服务商与 IP 类型筛选，每页最多展示 20 台主机。紫色住宅、蓝色 ISP 与灰色机房标签描述网络类型；文字与颜色不代表质量排名，也不保证 Claude 兼容性。浏览不会预留槽位。领取时请先登录，再通过浏览可用槽位，选择主机、核对月费，再确认账号页显示的购买。

Alpha pricing. Each machine has its own monthly USD rate in the public slot catalogue. Alpha pricing passes through recorded VPS and exit-IP costs, plus a monthly service fee of 10% of resource cost, with a $2 minimum per slot. Your chosen account service is billed separately from the slot: your own Claude plan for Anthropic, or your own Devin subscription for the pilot.

Recorded costs are normalized to a calendar month. A 30-day cost is multiplied by 365/360; an annual cost is divided by 12. For a customer quote, round the normalized USD resource cost to cents, half up; calculate 10% of that amount, round the service fee half up to cents with a $2 minimum, then add the two displayed amounts. Resource costs of $8.68, $10.83, $22.28 and $27.44 therefore give totals of $10.68, $12.83, $24.51 and $30.18 per month. The fee remains $2 through $20 of resource cost.

Alpha 定价。每台主机的美元月费分别列于公开槽位目录。按已记录的 VPS 与出口 IP 成本计价，另收资源成本 10% 的月度服务费，每槽位最低 $2。所选账号服务与槽位分别付费：Anthropic 路线使用你自己的 Claude 套餐，试点使用你自己的 Devin 订阅。

已记录成本统一折算为日历月：30 天成本乘以 365/360，年付成本除以 12。报价先将折算后的美元资源成本四舍五入到美分，再取其 10% 并将服务费四舍五入到美分，服务费最低 $2，最后将两项显示金额相加。资源成本 $8.68、$10.83、$22.28 和 $27.44，分别对应月费 $10.68、$12.83、$24.51 和 $30.18。资源成本不超过 $20 时，服务费为 $2。

The public breakdown shows VPS cost, separately recorded exit-IP cost, service fee and total. When hosting includes the exit IP, it shows the recorded bundled resource cost without inventing a split. A normalization rounding line explains any cent-level difference between individually rounded components and the rounded combined resource cost. Missing costs or costs not converted to USD make pricing unavailable. The host and IP-provider brands remain visible. ISP, Residential and Datacenter describe network types, with the same pricing formula and no separate residential premium; no category guarantees quality, speed or Claude compatibility.

公开明细展示 VPS 成本、单独记录的出口 IP 成本、服务费与总价。主机包含出口 IP 时，展示已记录的打包资源成本，不虚构拆分金额。若各项单独舍入后与合计资源成本存在美分差异，会以折算舍入行解释。成本缺失或尚未转换为美元时，价格暂不可用。主机与 IP 服务商品牌保持可见。ISP、住宅与机房只描述网络类型，所有类型采用同一公式，不另加住宅溢价；这些类型不保证质量、速度或 Claude 兼容性。

Alpha — Early Access. The service is still being tested and has not launched commercially. The operator has personally used one residential slot with encouraging results; reliability across providers, configurations and multiple users is still being evaluated. Alpha pricing is introductory and may change for future billing periods. You will receive an updated quote before renewal if pricing changes; an already agreed billing period retains its agreed price.

Alpha — 早期体验。服务仍在测试，尚未正式商业发布。运营者亲自使用过一个住宅槽位，结果令人鼓舞，但不同服务商、配置及多用户场景的可靠性仍待评估。Alpha 价格为初期价格，未来计费周期可能调整。如有变更，续期前会提供更新报价；已约定计费周期仍执行原约定价格。

Your account shows the payment choices available to you. Follow the payment steps above: credit purchases pay for one month, while an explicitly chosen monthly plan renews until canceled. Existing arrangements are kept.

账号页显示你可用的付费方式，操作见上方付费步骤：余额购买只支付一个月，明确选择的月付计划则续费到取消为止。已有安排保持不变。

Retired machines are removed from the rental catalogue. A temporarily offline machine that remains in service still appears on Status. Retirement does not release an existing holder or delete their account and payment history.

已退役主机会从出租目录移除。仍在运营、只是暂时离线的主机继续显示在服务状态中。退役不会自动归还已有持有者的槽位，也不会删除其账号或付款历史。

Each customer receives a dedicated VPS and dedicated exit IP. An unallocated slot is labelled Available; this describes availability, not a zero price. From Your slots, open Browse available slots to choose a priced machine and review its purchase confirmation.

每位客户获得专属 VPS 与专属出口 IP。未分配的槽位标为可用，表示可领取，不表示免费。从我的槽位打开浏览可用槽位，选择可用且已定价的主机，再核对购买确认页。

The user guide explains setup and the displayed quote. Terms describe agreed periods and slot access.

使用指南说明安装和报价明细，服务条款说明约定周期与槽位访问。

Your slots keeps your held slots, controls and usage together. To add a slot, choose Browse available slots. This signed-in page lets you search by host provider, IP provider or public machine label such as Machine 7, and filter by provider and network type (ISP, Residential or Datacenter). Results appear in pages of up to 20 machines. Review a machine’s price and cost breakdown, then choose that machine and follow its confirmation; you return to Your slots to manage it. A credit purchase spends the quoted balance once after you confirm; browsing never spends credit. Accounts using the earlier allowance keep their existing arrangement.

我的槽位集中展示你持有的槽位、操作和用量。需要增加槽位时，选择浏览可用槽位。此页面需要登录，可以按主机服务商、IP 服务商或“主机 7”等公开主机标签搜索，并按服务商及网络类型（ISP、住宅或机房）筛选；每页最多展示 20 台主机。先核对主机价格与成本明细，再按页面提示选择该主机，随后返回“我的槽位”管理。使用服务余额时，确认购买后只按报价扣一次余额，浏览不会扣费；原有额度模式保留原有安排。
